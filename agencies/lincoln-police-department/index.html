Open Questions

  1. The public dashboard listed 68 active fleet devices and zero outpost or lightpost devices on September 14, 2026. Does LPD operate or plan any fixed ALPR installations not reflected in that dashboard, and what non-tactical records describe their approval or operation?
  2. LPD reports are publicly listed for 2024, Q1–Q4 2025, and Q1–Q2 2026. How do those reporting periods relate to the annual-report requirement in General Order 2080 and Neb. Rev. Stat. § 60-3206?
  3. What current written data-sharing or system-access agreements govern LPD ALPR information sharing with Lancaster County Sheriff's Office, Nebraska State Patrol, Lincoln Parking Services, other governmental entities, or vendors?
  4. The LPD-linked transparency portal identifies Axon. What contracts, subscriptions, resellers, integrators, maintenance providers, or other vendors support LPD's ALPR program?
  5. What historical policy changes occurred between the November 1, 2024 and January 1, 2025 versions of General Order 2080?
  6. How do the dashboard's 'retained plate reads,' 'hot-list hits,' and 30-day category counts relate to the § 60-3206 report's confirmed-match and non-correlated-match fields?

Scope and limitations: This project seeks public administrative and accountability records relating to ALPR systems, including policies, procurement, installation, retention, statutory reporting, audits, and information-sharing agreements. It does not seek to publish raw license-plate capture data or personal information protected from public disclosure.

Public records requests filed under the Nebraska Public Records Act, Neb. Rev. Stat. §§ 84-712 through 84-712.09. Requests submitted via MuckRock. Last updated: September 24, 2026.
