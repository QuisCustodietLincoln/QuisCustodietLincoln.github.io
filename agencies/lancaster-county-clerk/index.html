Open Questions

  1. The NDOT State/County Agreement (C-23-0809) authorizes ALPR installation on state highway right-of-way. Where exactly are fixed readers installed, and how many?
  2. The December 2025 Motorola procurement ($793,715.71 total) covers 12 fixed solar/cellular L6Q cameras on a 5-year subscription. Has deployment begun and which highway corridors are covered?
  3. Has the Lancaster County Sheriff's Office submitted annual § 60-3206 reports to the Nebraska Crime Commission as required by the ALPR Privacy Act?
  4. Does Lancaster County share ALPR data with LPD, Nebraska State Patrol, or federal task forces under the Vigilant Solutions LEARN database access included in contract C-21-0724?

Scope and limitations: This project seeks public administrative and accountability records relating to ALPR systems, including policies, procurement, installation, retention, statutory reporting, audits, and information-sharing agreements. It does not seek to publish raw license-plate capture data or personal information protected from public disclosure.

Public records requests filed under the Nebraska Public Records Act, Neb. Rev. Stat. §§ 84-712 through 84-712.09. Requests submitted via MuckRock. Last updated: September 24, 2026.
