Nebraska ALPR Transparency Project

Quis Custodiet Nebraska

A public interest project tracking requests filed under the Nebraska Public Records Act to document how local and state agencies in Nebraska acquire, operate, and oversee automated license plate reader (ALPR) systems. Request materials, agency responses, and released records are published here as they are received.
