Public University Police Department

For campus police departments at Nebraska public universities that operate or access ALPR systems, including integration with parking enforcement.

Records sought

  • All current and superseded ALPR/LPR policies, standard operating procedures, privacy policies, retention schedules, training materials, and audit procedures governing campus police or university parking enforcement use of ALPR systems.
  • All contracts, subscription agreements, purchase orders, amendments, renewals, invoices, payment records, and vendor correspondence concerning ALPR/LPR equipment or services used by the university police department or parking division.
  • Current and historical inventory of all ALPR/LPR equipment used by the department or parking division, including vendor, model, asset identifier, fixed or mobile designation, assigned vehicle or installation location, and installation, removal, or replacement dates.
  • All records concerning integration between the university's ALPR/LPR system and parking permit enforcement, including system access rights, data flows, and vendor agreements.
  • All memoranda of understanding, data-sharing agreements, access agreements, and interagency correspondence involving ALPR/LPR information shared with or received from local, county, or state law enforcement agencies.
  • All existing aggregate or de-identified reports, dashboards, exports, and statistics concerning ALPR/LPR scans, alerts, matches, queries, user access, exports, and retention or deletion.
  • All annual ALPR/LPR practice and usage reports prepared or submitted under Neb. Rev. Stat. § 60-3206, if applicable.
  • Complaints, internal reviews, misuse investigations, corrective-action records, and audit findings concerning ALPR/LPR access, use, or data sharing.
