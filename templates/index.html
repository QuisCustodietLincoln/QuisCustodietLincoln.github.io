Request Templates

Model public records request letters for filing under the Nebraska Public Records Act, organized by agency type. Adapt for the specific agency and date range before filing. Submit via MuckRock or directly to the agency's designated records custodian.

Templates by Agency Type

Municipal Police Department
Policy, contracts, equipment inventory, audits, aggregate usage statistics, data-sharing agreements
County Sheriff's Office
Mobile and fixed systems, vehicle assignment, deployment, § 60-3206 reporting, NDOT agreements, policy
City Clerk / City Council
Council agendas, resolutions, approval history, public contracts, appropriations
County Clerk / County Board
Board minutes, contracts, claims, NDOT right-of-way agreements, procurement approvals
State Agency
Nebraska State Patrol, NDOT, Nebraska Crime Commission, state procurement and interagency records
Public University Police Department
Campus ALPR use, parking integration, policy, data-sharing, procurement

Scope and limitations: This project seeks public administrative and accountability records relating to ALPR systems, including policies, procurement, installation, retention, statutory reporting, audits, and information-sharing agreements. It does not seek to publish raw license-plate capture data or personal information protected from public disclosure.

Public records requests filed under the Nebraska Public Records Act, Neb. Rev. Stat. §§ 84-712 through 84-712.09. Requests submitted via MuckRock. Last updated: September 10, 2026.
