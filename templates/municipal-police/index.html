Municipal Police Department

For city and village police departments operating or accessing ALPR systems.

Records sought

  • Current and superseded ALPR/LPR policies, general orders, standard operating procedures, privacy policies, retention schedules, field guides, training materials, audit procedures, and policy-revision or approval records.
  • Annual ALPR/LPR practice and usage reports, attachments, corrections, and related correspondence prepared or submitted under Neb. Rev. Stat. § 60-3206.
  • All contracts, subscription agreements, statements of work, purchase orders, amendments, renewals, invoices, payment records, grant applications, grant awards, procurement records, bid materials, vendor proposals, and vendor correspondence concerning ALPR/LPR equipment or services.
  • Current and historical equipment inventory, including vendor, model, asset or system identifier, fixed or mobile designation, assigned vehicle or installation location, and installation, removal, replacement, or reassignment dates.
  • For fixed installations, all existing site-location records in the most specific non-exempt form maintained by the city.
  • Existing aggregate or de-identified reports, dashboards, exports, and statistics concerning ALPR/LPR scans, alerts, matches, queries, user access, exports, retention/deletion, and information sharing.
  • All memoranda of understanding, data-sharing agreements, access agreements, interagency correspondence, and system-integration records concerning ALPR/LPR information.
  • Complaints, internal reviews, misuse investigations, discipline or corrective-action records, and audit findings concerning ALPR/LPR access, use, dissemination, retention, or sharing.
